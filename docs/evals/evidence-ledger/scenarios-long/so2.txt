Schedulo report "State of Clinic Scheduling 2026"

Published by the Schedulo Insights Team, Austin, Texas.

Welcome to this year's report. Since our last edition, the Schedulo team has grown to 48 people across offices in Austin and Lisbon, and our support desk now operates from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Central on weekdays. We also moved our documentation to a new help center, so older bookmarks may need updating. Thank you to the customers and partners who sent feedback on last year's layout. Inside you will find sections on front-desk staffing, reminder message preferences, and room utilization, along with a short glossary of terms used throughout.

Key finding: 60% of mid-size clinics plan to switch scheduling software within 12 months. Methodology: online poll of 212 registrants of Schedulo's "Time to Switch?" webinar series.

Front-desk notes: reception teams we spoke with continue to favor text reminders over voice calls. Several practices mentioned that color-coded calendars help new staff learn the daily layout faster. Room utilization charts appear on pages 14 through 19.

Glossary: "No-show" refers to a booked appointment the patient did not attend without notice. "Buffer" means time blocked between appointments for cleaning or charting. "Template" refers to a reusable weekly schedule pattern for a provider or room.

Upcoming: Schedulo will host its annual customer meetup in Lisbon on March 18, 2027, and registration opens in January. Press inquiries can be sent to our communications team through the contact form on our website. This report may be shared freely with attribution.

== D6: National Association of Ambulatory Clinics, annual member census
National Association of Ambulatory Clinics, annual member census

This year's census report is being distributed to members ahead of the autumn regional meetings. Printed copies will be available at the registration desk in each host city, and the full tables can be requested through the member services office. We thank the volunteers from the Northeast and Mountain chapters who helped assemble the mailing lists and answered member questions by phone during the collection period. Staffing results this year show median front-desk headcount holding steady at four positions, while the share of members reporting at least one unfilled nursing role rose to 38%.

1,840 responding mid-size clinics (response rate 71%). 11% report plans to replace scheduling software in the next 12 months. 23% say "within three years".

Elsewhere in the census, 64% of members reported adding at least one exam room since the previous year, and average weekly operating hours rose slightly to 52. Parking and patient transport remain the most frequently cited facility concerns in rural chapters. Dues for the coming year will remain unchanged at the current tier rates, and the board has approved a small increase in the travel stipend for chapter delegates. The spring policy forum will be held in Columbus; hotel blocks open in January. Members with corrections to their listed contact details or chapter assignment should write to member services before the end of the quarter so the directory can be updated in time for printing.
