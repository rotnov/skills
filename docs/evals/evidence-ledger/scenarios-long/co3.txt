== D6: Product requirements, pricing policy
Product requirements, pricing policy

Owner: Commerce product team. Last reviewed in the quarterly policy sync. Questions go to the #pricing-policy channel.

Scope. This document covers how prices are presented across the storefront, the mobile apps and partner widgets. It does not cover supplier cost negotiations, wholesale agreements or internal margin targets, which live in the finance handbook.

Currency and formatting. Prices are shown in the shopper's selected currency with the local symbol and decimal separator. Amounts are rounded to two decimal places, except for currencies that have no minor unit, such as the yen. Where tax is included by law, the label "incl. VAT" appears next to the amount.

Displayed prices must reflect the current price list. Flash-sale prices change on the minute, and showing a stale price is a compliance issue: the advertised price must match checkout.

Accessibility. Price text must meet contrast guidelines and be readable by screen readers, including the currency name rather than only the symbol. Strike-through reference prices need an accessible label such as "was" so the comparison is announced.

Bundles and gift cards. Bundle pricing is shown as a single total with a link to the itemized breakdown. Gift card denominations are fixed and are not eligible for promotional codes.

Open items. Design is still finalizing the badge style for member-only offers, and legal will confirm wording for the loyalty points disclaimer before the holiday campaign. Feedback on this draft is welcome until the end of the month.
