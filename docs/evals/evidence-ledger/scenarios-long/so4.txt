== D1: Lumen paper, abstract and setup section
Lumen: A Note on Optimizer Behavior in Compact Language Models

Preprint, version 2. Correspondence should be directed to the first author via the contact form on the project page. This version corrects several typographical errors in the appendix tables, fixes a broken citation in the related-work section, and updates the author list to reflect a change in affiliation for the third author, who has since moved to a different institution.

Abstract and Setup

Throughout this paper we use lowercase bold letters for vectors and uppercase bold letters for matrices. Scalars are written in plain italics in the typeset version. The symbol t denotes the step index, and all logarithms are natural unless stated otherwise. Figures are best viewed in color, though grayscale-friendly patterns have been added for print readers.

Lumen reaches 6.1% lower validation loss than AdamW on four small-transformer tasks. The AdamW baseline used the default learning rate 1e-3 with no sweep. Logs and checkpoints are released.

The remainder of the paper is organized as follows. Section 2 reviews related work on optimization for neural networks. Section 3 introduces notation in more detail and describes the update rule. Section 4 presents the experimental results, and Section 5 offers a brief discussion of limitations and open questions. Appendices contain extended tables, additional figures, and a short glossary of terms.

Acknowledgments. We thank the members of our reading group for helpful conversations during the drafting of this manuscript, and the anonymous workshop reviewers for comments on an earlier abstract. The paper template is adapted from a publicly available conference style file.

== D5: Workshop paper by an unaffiliated university group
Submitted to the Workshop on Reproducibility in Machine Learning, non-archival track. This short paper was prepared by a small group of graduate students and one postdoctoral researcher as part of a reading-group project that ran during the spring term, alongside regular teaching duties. We thank the department's research computing staff for patiently handling our queue requests, and the campus library for interlibrary loan access to two older textbooks on numerical methods that we consulted while writing the background notes. Page limits for this track are four pages excluding references, so several appendices have been moved to the companion repository.

We re-ran Lumen and AdamW from scratch on the same four tasks with 5 seeds, sweeping the learning rate for both. With tuned AdamW (lr 3e-4) the gap is 0.4% plus or minus 0.9%, not significant. Lumen costs 18% more wall-clock time per step.

Code, configuration files, and plotting scripts will be released under an MIT license once the anonymity period ends. Until then, reviewers can access a read-only mirror through the link provided in the submission form. Questions about the build environment are best directed to the first author, who maintains the container recipes. Two of us will present a poster during the afternoon session on the second day of the workshop, and we would welcome conversations about extending this reading-group format to other courses. A recorded five-minute lightning talk will also be uploaded to the workshop's video page. Finally, we are grateful to the anonymous reviewers of an earlier draft, whose comments on figure readability and citation formatting substantially improved the presentation.
== D6: Our pipeline note
Our pipeline note

Last updated by the infra rotation. This page collects the settings and conventions for the small-transformer training pipeline so new people do not have to dig through old chat threads. If something here is wrong, fix it directly and mention the change in the team channel.

Data: the cleaned web-and-code mix from the March snapshot, deduplicated at the document level. Tokenizer is the shared 32k BPE vocabulary; do not regenerate it locally. Shards live in the team bucket under the datasets prefix, and the loader reads them in a fixed order seeded by the run name.

Model: 12 layers, hidden size 768, rotary position embeddings, pre-norm.

Optimizer: AdamW, lr 3e-4, tuned by sweep last year. Training budget is wall-clock bound: 40 GPU-hours per run.

Checkpoints: saved every 2,000 steps to the shared bucket, keeping the five most recent plus any you tag manually. Logging goes to the team dashboard; please name runs as owner-date-shortdescription so the list stays sortable.

Housekeeping: the node pool is shared with the speech group on Tuesdays and Thursdays, so queue long jobs early in the week. Config files belong in the repo, not in personal folders. Questions about storage quotas go to Priya; questions about the data loader go to Marco. The next cleanup of stale checkpoints is scheduled for the first Monday of the month. Please also delete scratch runs you no longer need once their logs are archived.
