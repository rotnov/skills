== D1: Salvo case study, home-goods retailer
A home-goods retailer with 1.2 million loyalty members tested Salvo for six weeks. Half of each send used Salvo personalisation, half the existing subject lines. Open rate rose from 18.1% to 22.3% (a lift of 23%). The retailer's loyalty programme collects full name, birthday and postcode at sign-up, so profiles were complete. Sends went out on Tuesdays and Saturdays at 10:00 local time. Unsubscribe rate did not change.
== D2: Agency write-up, travel brand
Our agency ran a holdout test of Salvo for a travel client: 400k subscribers who had booked at least once, 50/50 split, eight sends. Opens up 21%, clicks up 9%. Booking records gave us clean first names for the whole audience. The creative team liked that the tool needed no template changes. Setup took two days including the data-processing agreement.
== D3: Conference talk summary, grocery delivery company
Speaker from a grocery delivery company presented an in-house A/B test of Salvo on the weekly offers email: 2.1 million app users, four weeks, +22% opens. Audience question about data quality: every account has a first name because it is required at checkout. The speaker's main advice was to keep preheader text short. Slides are available on request.
== D4: Salvo product documentation, how personalisation works
Salvo rewrites the subject line to open with the recipient's first name and adjusts the wording around it. The first_name field is required for personalisation. For a recipient with no first_name value, Salvo sends the campaign's default subject line unchanged. Other profile fields (city, last purchase) are optional and only used in the email body. Reports break results down by personalised and default sends.
== D5: Our CRM data audit (last quarter)
List size 86,400 contacts. Source of contacts: 64% trade-show badge scans and webinar sign-ups that capture a work email and company only; 36% account sign-ups with full profile. Field completeness: email 100%, company 93%, first name 29%, job title 41%. Bounce rate 1.2%. Current open rate 24%.
